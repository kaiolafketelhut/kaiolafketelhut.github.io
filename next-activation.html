NeXT Activation Functions - by OMQ
From Dandes Academy Reel

1
Sigmoid
1 / (1 + e^-x) • range 0 to 1
Turns scores into probabilities
▶ Watch on YouTube - Sigmoid
2
Tanh
tanh(x) • range -1 to 1
Zero-centred, inside RNNs & LSTMs
▶ Watch on YouTube - Tanh
3
ReLU
max(0, x) • range 0 to ∞
Fast default for hidden layers
▶ Watch on YouTube - ReLU
4
Leaky ReLU
max(αx, x) • α = 0.1, fixed
A small leak keeps neurons alive
▶ Watch on YouTube - Leaky ReLU
5
PReLU
max(αx, x) • α learned
The network learns the leak
▶ Watch on YouTube - PReLU
6
ELU
x>0 ? x : α(e^x-1) • α = 1
Smooth curve, floors at -1
▶ Watch on YouTube - ELU
7
SiLU / Swish
x • σ(x) • dips just below 0
Powers SwiGLU in LLaMA-style LLMs
▶ Watch on YouTube - SiLU / Swish
8
GELU
x • Φ(x) • Φ = normal CDF
Used in BERT, GPT-2 & ViT
▶ Watch on YouTube - GELU
